How the implied value is derived

Every protocol on this board records no token in the snapshot. Its implied value is what a token would be worth if the protocol's record stayed as it is. It is computed, never assigned by hand:

implied value = TVL × category multiple × momentum × maturity

Category multiple
The median market-cap / TVL of the snapshot's own traded protocols in the same category — the board is priced from the snapshot's comparables, not from an outside list. Thin categories are shrunk toward the global median (prior strength 4) and every multiple is clamped to [0.02, 3]. The fitted table is below.
Momentum
From the 7-day TVL change: multiplier 1 + 0.6 × change, with the change clamped to [−50%, +100%], so one hot week can lift a value by at most 60% and a bad week cannot erase it.
Maturity
From the days the protocol has been tracked with no token: 0.55 for a brand-new listing, rising linearly to 1.00 at 1,460 days. A protocol with no listing date is treated as 730 days old. Time survived without a token is evidence the launch, when it comes, is a considered one.

Scope of the board

The board is the 40-protocol watchlist (pre-token, TVL at or above the $50M floor) plus every pre-token protocol below the floor — the early finds a radar exists for. Protocols above the floor that are not on the watchlist are wrapped, staked or bridged products of tokens that already trade; they are not launch candidates and appear under Comparables instead, where their real market caps calibrate the model.
